Physics Lab Cart

Data Dictionary

Time (s)
numeric, time in seconds since start of experiment.
Force (N)
numeric, force in newtons measured by the bumper
Position (m)
numeric, calculated position, relative to start of experiment
Velocity (m/s)
numeric, calculated velocity in meters per second in the direction of motion
Acceleration (m/s²)
numeric, measured acceleration in meters per second squared in the direction of motion
K_e (J)
numeric, calculated kinetic energy in Joules.
U_g (J)
numeric, calculated gravitational potential energy in Joules.
E (J)
numeric, total mechanic energy (U_g + K_e) in Joules.

The Experiments

  • A wheeled cart with a spring bumper on one side is placed on a horizontal track.
  • The cart is given a push. It coasts until its bumper collides with a stopper at one end of the track and reverses direction.
  • The process repeats until the cart comes to a stop.

  • A wheeled cart with a spring bumper on one end is placed at the top of an inclined ramp.
  • When released, the cart accelerates in the downward direction.
  • At the bottom of the ramp, the cart's spring bumper collides with a stopper, the cart reverses direction up the ramp until it slows and begins moving down again.
  • The process continues until the carts speed reaches 0.

Files

Check out the last file management tutorial to see how to extrac the files out of a zip archive.

  • cart_data.zip, zip archive containing two files associated with an experiment:
    • cart_flat.csv, data recorded while cart was in motion on a flat track
    • cart_incline.csv, data recorded while cart was in motion on an inclined track
  • cart_incline_energy.csv, data file from energy experiment.
  • cart_incline_tiny.csv, a tiny version of cart incline data for demos and learning.

Instrumentation

The cart has built in accelerometers, a kind of sensor that measure the force exerted by a known mass. This information is sufficient to calculate acceleration = force / mass. All other data, such as velocity and position, are calculated by the software running the experiment before outputing to the data file.
