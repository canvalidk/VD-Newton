## Working understanding of the vision

The VD aims to make the procedure by which a theory produces predictions explicit enough to inspect, compare, challenge, and reproduce. Its subject includes the concepts, recognitions, modelling choices, operations, and contextual commitments that actually enter prediction-making.

The newer auditability introduction adds an important motivation: many objects that participate in prediction-making are unnamed. Naming and assembling them makes them available for scrutiny and makes their interactions available for discovery. Its three success criteria are perceptiveness, reliability, and scale.

Theory text is itself a causal object. Exposure to a particular definition or residual can change what a trained interpreter does. The project therefore cares about the route that produces an answer, as well as the answer. Education, a Newton abacus, and a trace/evaluator tool are applications and experimental surfaces for that broader aim.

There are several related layers, whose claims should stay distinguishable:

- Theory develops the account of meaning, handles, admission, authority, and prediction-making.
- Core specifies the dictionary/log and law/house architecture.
- Code makes structural bookkeeping and encounter recording tractable.
- Newton provides a demanding worked instance: it exposes what is otherwise silently imported when a familiar mechanics answer is produced.
- Empirical work must establish how reliably the resulting text routes actual interpreters.

The broad cognitive-procedure description and the empirical causal-text description fit together as a research direction. Exactly which claims are descriptive, normative, or experimentally established remains a useful question for later discussion.

## Traces

A run proceeds through a normalised question or goal expression, selected entries, demanded daughter headwords, residual presentations, supplied inputs, interpreter responses, and a value or an informative stopping point.

The trace records the answer-production route. It is not merely a retrospective justification written after obtaining a textbook answer. Laziness matters because the sequence of exposures participates in producing the response.

The September 1 function-call decisions are essential to reading earlier material correctly:

1. Expansion selects an entry.
2. Beta-like instantiation substitutes actuals and constructs the residual stimulus.
3. R-reduction records one person's observed response.

There is no assumed primitive delta-reduction that gives the residual built-in arithmetic or physical meaning. An R-response is not automatically a certified equivalent rewrite. One trace records one encounter; response probabilities are inferred across encounters, relative to the person/population, presentation, context, and admissibility conditions.

The current simplified response protocol retains terminal inert response text and conditions observations on admissible input. It is explicitly provisional. A trace being complete and transparent does not alone establish semantic correctness, physical truth, or population-level reliability.

The useful output includes the nature of a halt: missing contingent input, unresolved variable, undefined referent, domain boundary, contradictory exact premises, empirical incompatibility, or non-identification. These are not interchangeable.

Mechanical bookkeeping can record what entry was selected, what text was supplied, and where a response entered. Recognition, choice of model, experimental warrant, and the reliability of the response still require an account. A correct numerical endpoint alone does not show that the intended VD route produced it.

## Residues, handles, and dossiers

Several senses must be kept apart.

| Sense | What it contributes |
| --- | --- |
| Residual text/template | The literal structure around recognised headword occurrences. In the code representation, a residual contains text segments separated by ordered holes. |
| Operational handle | The repeatable route by which that presentation engages an interpreter's installed operation. |
| Encounter/effect | What this particular exposure actually elicited from this target in this context. |
| Idealised residual function | An abstraction for the transformation the entry is intended to elicit; not a guarantee built into the string. |
| Physical discrepancy | For example, a mismatch between an observed net force and an accounted sum. This uses the word residual in a different sense. |

The handle derivation begins with matching two unknown interpreters without making a known reference interpreter the hidden standard. Its proposed forcing move parameterises the reference-dependent operational positions. The subsequent dossier account makes matching conditional on accessible probes, target discipline, chosen output coarse-graining, and an accepted mismatch budget.

A stimulant is the concrete causal presentation; a local handle routes the target; a dossier records responses and dispositions under probes. Discipline means sufficient stability for that record to support matching. Raw meaning is the convergent dossier content held at a handle. Literal identity of marks, equality of computed values, and agreement of operational dossiers are different relations.

This explains why residual prose is load-bearing. It may successfully route installed competence, misroute it, or silently demand competence that has never been installed. Good entry writing must identify which of those is happening. A novel modelling distinction cannot be assumed to exist merely because evocative prose mentions it.

The no-lookup d2 rule places a strong locality requirement on residual execution: local residual text, explicitly delivered arguments, and installed external competence are available; the rest of the dictionary and earlier trace memory are not implicit sources. Argument delivery supplies what would otherwise be smuggled in by recall. The precise scope and empirical enforceability of installed competence remain important.

## Dictionary authority, laws, and houses

The named dictionary makes the authority behind a definitional claim explicit. A definition is a particular string in that authority; its causal meaning is a separate question. Multiple entries for a headword belong to an append-only history. A choice among entries needs to be visible rather than silently identified with a latest-wins dictionary function.

In the current working Core architecture, each law corner jointly relates the other two heads through its own residual. A directed hyperedge is more faithful than an ordinary pairwise triangle. The three inward entries sit in a six-entry functional house: the prior November chimney, the three inward corners, and two outward winter walls. Houses can overlap.

The admission note gives the intended semantic roles: previously available candidate/phenomenon, newly admitted kind/result, and co-admitted differentiator. It distinguishes this local admission argument from the stronger claim that every irreducible law-bearing enlargement normalises to exactly that form. It explicitly does not derive the global unique-November restriction.

The authority-document note explores complete renaming and name-free structure but says it has not derived the full VD architecture. The Haskell analogy and the acyclic confluence note are narrower still: neither proves the full law/trace system confluent or makes observed R-responses truth-preserving. The acyclic note requires its simplifying rewrite assumptions; multiple entry choices and empirical responses cannot simply be folded into its result.

## Newton as a test of the vision

Four areas show what the project is trying to make visible.

**Scene to target.** A trajectory needs a re-identifiable referent. The recent scene-trackability note begins with a scene and qualified access, not pre-given bodies. It separates observable coordinates from inertial certification, addresses coverage and instrument limits, and distinguishes a tracking proxy from the material continuity it is supposed to track. The older lumping note then considers redundancy, tolerance, non-unique clique choices, and the creation of a particle/mass address. Address creation is not mass-value determination.

**Force membership and closure.** The August 26 local ratification is net-force / impressed-force / interaction-set. The set contains identity-bearing interactions; each interaction supplies a target-directed impressed-force contribution. Equal vector values do not identify the interactions. An aggregate sum does not supply an individual membership rule, and agreement of a sum with a measured net force does not certify completeness. Empty, unknown, and incompletely enumerated are different states.

**Mass determination.** Exact positive-mass Newton II allows a uniquely identified mass, compatible zero-zero non-identification, or incompatible exact premises. Empirical inputs retain their uncertainty and provenance and require a joint inference under a declared policy. The original measurements, discrepancy, fit standing, and policy must survive. A model-compatible fitted pair cannot be reported as independent confirmation of the relation used to construct it.

The current estimator synthesis separates an absolute compatibility/rejection gate from the conditional mass readout. The September reviews challenge parts of the earlier justification and propose stronger composition arguments. Their uniqueness argument is presented with a proof sketch and assumptions; the gate calibration has regime qualifications. Reading these reviews is not an independent validation of every theorem, numerical result, or proposed generalisation.

**Prediction sets and missing structure.** A failed prediction can put pressure on inputs, frame, force inventory, approximation, object persistence, interpreter behaviour, or the theory. The proposed prediction-set project asks which repair branches the actual trace licenses and what further evidence each demands. It does not authorise arbitrary theory-saving excuses. The typed closure-residual work is a proposed extension that explicitly presumes entries and witness machinery not yet fully encoded.

## Questions retained for the later discussion

These are discussion axes, not a conclusion that the project lacks an answer.

1. What exact practitioner, training state, context, and task distribution is the VD claiming to represent?
2. What evidence would distinguish a residual that causes the intended operation from one that merely accompanies a solver already doing the work from memory?
3. What should a dossier record to support reliable comparison across interpreters, including failed or inadmissible responses?
4. How should installed external competence, question normalisation, entry selection, and residual locality be delimited and tested?
5. Which structural rules are definitions of the chosen framework, which are independently justified constraints, and which are universal hypotheses still awaiting counterexample tests?
6. What observable success would establish the discovery ambition beyond finding deficiencies in existing notation or entry wording?
7. How can prediction-set repair and halt decisions remain accountable while an investigation is incomplete, without making either premature theory rejection or unlimited protective repair automatic?
