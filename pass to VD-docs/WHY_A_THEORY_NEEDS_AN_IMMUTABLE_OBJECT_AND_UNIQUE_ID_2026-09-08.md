## The hypothesis and the theory are different objects

In the distinction developed here, a **hypothesis** is a particular claim submitted to a test. A **theory** is the continuing structure that generates hypotheses from specified inputs and assumptions.

That difference becomes essential when we move between experiments. We generate a hypothesis, test it, then generate another hypothesis and test that. The hypotheses can differ, yet we claim that both experiments teach us something about **one theory**. What remains the same across those tests is the structure responsible for their predictions.

The crucial connection is therefore:

> The outcome assesses a particular hypothesis. Its evidential significance for the theory depends on the theory's identifiable commitment to that hypothesis.

This is what allows the learning to extend beyond the individual tested claim. We are assessing a structure that also generates further, possibly untested hypotheses. The amount and direction of that evidential update depend on the predictions and the evidential model.

## Why the structure must be immutable

**Immutability preserves the object receiving that accumulated assessment.** If the generating structure changes unnoticed between experiments, we have tested hypotheses produced by different structures. We cannot simply treat their results as the predictive record of one unchanged theory.

Different inputs can produce different hypotheses; the rules connecting inputs to predictions must remain fixed for that identified theory instance.

## Why the name or ID must be unique

**A unique ID makes that continuing object addressable.** Each hypothesis's derivation can identify which theory generated it, and each assessment can refer back to that same theory.

The ID connects separate tests to their common source. Its permanent association with an immutable structure ensures that the source has not been substituted.

## What this requires of an Authority Dictionary

An Authority Dictionary representing a theory needs both properties. Its entries and their organization preserve the structure that licenses hypotheses. Its unique identifier lets those hypotheses—and the evidence bearing on them—refer to that particular structure.

**The hypothesis is the commitment tested on an occasion. The theory is the enduring source of commitments whose assessment can change across occasions.** Representing that enduring source as an immutable, uniquely identified object makes the claimed continuity of scientific learning inspectable.
