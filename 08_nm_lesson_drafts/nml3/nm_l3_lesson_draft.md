# NML3 Lesson Draft: Force Sum

Status: scaffold only. The triplet names are settled; lesson wording, entry
numbers, and the surrounding cut are not.

## Fixed purpose

Open the force-accounting route from the already-installed net-force entry and
establish the relation:

```text
net-force / impressed-force / interaction-set
```

For a target particle, the `interaction-set` supplies the relevant interactions;
each interaction supplies a target-directed `impressed-force`; and the vector
sum of those impressed forces is the target's `net-force`.

## Entry spine

The old E13–E17 spine was retired by the managed NML3 reset of 2026-08-17. Do
not assign the settled headwords to entry numbers here until the new cut is
decided.

## Lesson sections to draft

1. Opening demand: where a net-force value comes from when it is not supplied.
2. Collection/member distinction: `interaction-set` contains interactions.
3. Index/result distinction: each interaction supplies a target-directed
   `impressed-force` rather than being added as a vector itself.
4. Vector-sum operation after independent interaction-set closure.
5. Interaction admission and identity.
6. Closure, including why a matching net-force sum cannot exclude further
   interactions whose impressed forces cancel.
7. Empty interaction-set and zero-vector result.
8. Exit trace and evaluator checks.

These sections are candidates, not a settled wall or entry allocation.

## Boundaries

- Do not treat the interaction set as a flat unexplained human-supplied list.
- Do not infer membership or closure merely from aggregate agreement.
- Do not collapse distinct interactions whose impressed forces have equal
  vector values.
- Do not reinstate the retired E13–E17 allocation without a new structure
  decision.
- Do not settle action/reaction, concrete force laws, or later NML3 blocks in
  this naming pass.
