## Closure boundary

NML6 mechanical closure is not the same judgment as NML3 IFS closure.

- NML3 closes one target's force account by establishing that no further
  impressed-force member remains.
- NML6 asks whether the completed force accounts of selected system members
  pair into internal interactions or expose external connections.

NML6 therefore depends on trustworthy NML3 force-account membership and
closure, but does not replace them.
