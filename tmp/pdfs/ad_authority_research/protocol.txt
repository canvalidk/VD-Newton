Here  is  the  Valid  Dictionary  (VD)  Protocol  v1.0 .  
1.  The  Macro  Structure  (The  Object)  
The  Valid  Dictionary  is  not  a  static  book;  it  is  a  generic  state  machine  defined  by  an  ordered  
list
 
of
 
transactions.
 
 $$\mathcal{D}  =  (\text{Name},  \mathbf{L})$$  Where:  ●  $\mathcal{D}$  is  the  Dictionary  Instance  (e.g.,  "Newtonian  Mechanics  v1").  ●  $\text{Name}$  is  the  unique  identifier  string.  ●  $\mathbf{L}$  is  the  Append-Only  Log  of  entries.  
The  State  of  the  dictionary  at  time  $n$  is  defined  recursively:  
 $$\mathcal{D}^{(n)}  =  \mathcal{D}^{(n-1)}  +  E_n$$  
2.  The  Micro  Structure  (The  Entry)  
Each  entry  $E_n$  in  the  log  is  a  tuple  containing  a  Headword  ($w$)  and  a  Definitional  
Structure
 
($\Delta$).
 
 $$E_n  =  (w,  \Delta)$$  There  are  two  distinct  types  of  Entries  based  on  their  function  in  the  loop:  
A.  The  November  Entry  (The  Anchor)  This  is  a  word  that  connects  the  system  to  human  experience.  It  contains  Residue .  
 $$E_{\text{Nov}}  =  (w,  \Gamma)$$  ●  $w$:  The  word  (e.g.,  "Force",  "Heat").  ●  $\Gamma$  (Gamma):  The  Residue  String .  This  is  the  procedure  for  physical  recognition  
(e.g.,
 
"The
 
sensation

"The

B.  The  Law  Entry  (The  Triplet)  This  is  the  "Engine"  of  the  dictionary.  It  defines  a  relationship  between  a  November  word  and

 $$E_{\text{Law}}  =  \text{Triplet}(w_{\text{Nov}},  w_{\text{Jan}},  w_{\text{Feb}})$$  
3.  The  Topology  (The  Loop)  
This  is  your  specific  solution  to  the  circularity  problem.  A  "Law"  is  valid  if  and  only  if  it  forms  a  
closed
 
3-cycle
 
graph.
 
The  Law  Triplet  $\mathcal{T}$  is  defined  as:  
 $$\mathcal{T}  =  \langle  w_{\text{Nov}}  \to  w_{\text{Jan}}  \to  w_{\text{Feb}}  \to  w_{\text{Nov}}  
\rangle$$
 Where:  1.  $w_{\text{Nov}}$  (November):  The  Meaning  Donor  (Has  Residue  $\Gamma$).  2.  $w_{\text{Jan}}$  (January):  The  First  Structural  Slot  (e.g.,  Mass).  3.  $w_{\text{Feb}}$  (February):  The  Second  Structural  Slot  (e.g.,  Acceleration).  
The  Closure  Axiom:  
The  definition  of  $w_{\text{Nov}}$  depends  on  the  interaction  of  $w_{\text{Jan}}$  and  
$w_{\text{Feb}}$.
 
The  definition  of  $w_{\text{Jan}}$  depends  on  the  ratio  of  $w_{\text{Nov}}$  and  
$w_{\text{Feb}}$.
 
4.  The  Validity  Function  (The  Audit)  
To  determine  if  a  dictionary  is  "Valid"  (Sound),  we  apply  the  Boolean  function  
$\text{IsValid}(\mathcal{D})$.
 
A  dictionary  is  Valid  if,  for  every  Law  Triplet  $\mathcal{T}$  in  the  Log:  1.  Anchoring:  The  November  word  $w_{\text{Nov}}$  must  have  a  non-empty  Residue  
$\Gamma$. $$\Gamma(w_{\text{Nov}})  \neq  \emptyset$$  2.  Closure:  The  Winter  words  ($w_{\text{Jan}},  w_{\text{Feb}}$)  must  be  defined  strictly  in  
terms

\mathcal{D}^{(n-1)}$$
