1.1 Motivation
Every VD entry stores its definition as a raw string. A definition typically

supplies some connective structure tying those headwords together. Theresidue

template-with-holes.

2 The Valid Dictionary (VD) Project
2.1 Science has an Audit Problem
The Problem.Physics demands rigorous precision in its mathematics (e.g.
F=ma), but accepts dangerous ambiguity in its language. We treat scientific
theories as static “books” of facts, ignoring the order in which concepts are
built. This leads toConcept Rot: students attempt to understand high-level
abstractions (like “Energy” or “Fields”) without understanding the foundational
observations that ground them.
The Solution.TheValid Dictionary(VD) is a protocol that redefines a sci-
entific theory as anappend-only logof semantic transactions. It applies the logic
of software version control to the philosophy of science.
2.2 The 3 Core Rules
1.The Log.A theory is a sequence of events. You cannot use a “Future
Word” to define a “Past Word.”
2.The Anchor.Every chain of definitions must eventually hitResidue—a
direct human sensation or measurement (e.g. a dial reading).
3.The Loop (The Law Triplet).APhysical Lawis not a truth-claim;
it is aclosed 3-cycle. It locks three terms together (like Force, Mass,
Acceleration) to create a stable structure.
2.3 The Discovery
When we “audited” Newtonian Mechanics using this protocol, we discovered a
hidden hierarchy:
•Newton’s 2nd Law is alow-degreeloop (close to reality/sensation).
•Newton’s 3rd Law is ahigh-degreeloop (requires abstract bookkeeping
like “Systems” and “Momentum”).
2.4 The Goal
To create a standard forauditable science. By making dependencies explicit, we
can improve human education (teaching concepts in the right order) and enable
AI to reason about causality without hallucinating.

3 The Valid Dictionary Protocol
To move beyond the vague definition of “scientific theory,” we formalize a theory
as a named data structure governed by a strict set of rules. This protocol—the
Valid Dictionary(VD)—redefines a theory not as a static collection of facts,
but as a sequential log of semantic transactions.
3.1 The Dictionary Object
The foundational unit of analysis is theDictionary Instance. We define the
dictionaryDnot as a function, but as a named authority object consisting of a
unique identifier and an ordered history:
D= (N ame,L)
Where:
•Name: A unique string identifier (e.g.Newtonian Mechanics v1.0) that
establishes the scope of authority.
•L: TheEntry-Log, an ordered, append-only sequence of entries (E n)n≥0.
The “state” of a theory is not timeless; it is path-dependent. The state of
the dictionary at indexn, denotedD (n), is the cumulative result of all entries
E0 throughE n.
Consequently, the “definition” of any wordwis not a single output string,
but the complete history of transactions involvingwin the log.
3.2 The Entry Structure
Each transaction in the log is a tuple representing a definitional event:
En = (w, δ)
Components:
•w(Headword): The term being defined.
•δ(Definition String): The literal syntactic output.
Crucially, the VD distinguishes betweenSyntactic DefinitionandSemantic
Residue:
•Syntactic Definition (δ):A string definingwin terms of other words
in the log (e.g. “Net Force := Mass×Acceleration”).
•Residue (Γ):A special class of definition that points outside the log to a
human-executable procedure or recognition pattern (e.g. “The sensation
of muscle tension” or “The reading on a calibrated dial”). Residue is the
mechanism by which the abstract log grounds itself in physical reality.

3.3 The Structure of Physical Law: The Triplet
In standard discourse, a physical law is often treated as a sentence or equation.
In the VD protocol, aPhysical Lawis identified as a specific topological structure
within the log: a closed 3-cycle of mutually dependent entries.
We term this structure theLaw Triplet. It rigidly assigns roles to the three
terms involved to ensure logical stability without circular collapse.
The Roles
The Anchor Term (w Anc):
•Constraint:Must be previously defined (exists at indexk < n) and must
carry traceable Residue.
•Function:Serves as the structural bridge between the new law and the
existing log. It “donates” meaning to the cycle, preventing the law from
being purely tautological.
The Cyclic Terms (w Cyc1 , wCyc2):
•Constraint:Must not be previously defined (or are treated as empty
slots).
•Function:Acquire meaning solely through their structural relationship
with the Anchor Term.
The Topology
A Law Triplet is formed by three sequential entries (EA, EB, EC) that lock these
terms together:
wAnc :=f(w Cyc1 , wCyc2)
wCyc1 :=f(w Anc, wCyc2)
wCyc2 :=f(w Anc, wCyc1)
This structure formalizes theconstructive circularityof physics. The law
does not define the Cyclic Terms in isolation; it defines the relations between
them, anchored byw Anc.
3.4 Governance Rules
To ensure the integrity of the audit trail, the VD enforces three strict constraints:
Rule 1: The Law of Linear ProvenanceA definitionE n is valid if and
only if every term used in its definition stringδexists at an indexk < n, or is
a Cyclic Term introduced simultaneously within a Law Triplet.

4 Introduction
Formal understanding of the phenomenon of “scientific theories” and their role
in science remains either vague or incomplete. While physicists demand rigorous
precision in their mathematics, thetheoryitself—the vehicle that carries these
equations—is often treated as an intuitive, unstructured object. We aim to
rigorously identify the mathematical nature and behaviour of scientific theories
by analysing the provenance of their language.
In doing so, we developed a protocol of rules—theValid Dictionary(VD)—that
enables scientific theories to be expressed explicitly and auditably. The proto-
col identifies the theory-specific dictionary as a critical structural feature of any
theory, managing it not as a static list, but as anaudit trailof definition entries.
By encoding theories into this common framework, we can trace the lineage of
concepts and interrogate significant linguistic features of the laws. Importantly,
aPhysical Lawis identified not as a standalone equation, but as a structural
feature of this log: a triplet of interdependent definitions.
We apply this framework by distinguishing between abstract theories (such
as Newtonian Mechanics), which we callTheory Classes, and the specific ma-
terial used to teach them, which we callTheory Instances. This distinction is
central to our methodology. Just as a mechanic studies the abstract engineering
of a “Car” by examining a specific vehicle on a lift, we study the abstract nature
of a Theory Class by auditing a specific Theory Instance. While every specific
car carries a unique history of repairs and modifications—analogous to the spe-
cific diagrams and explanations added by a lecturer—the underlying engine (the
Dictionary structure) remains accessible for structural analysis.
We used the protocol to encode a simplified version of Newtonian Mechan-
ics, enabling a systematised discussion of its internal architecture. The results
demonstrate that by making these hidden structures explicit, we can not only
audit the logical consistency of a theory, but also provide concrete methods to
improve the clarity and quality of physics education.
